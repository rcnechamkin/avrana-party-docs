# Technical reference

You do not need this section to understand Avrana Party. The [Overview](../introduction/index.md),
[Architecture](../architecture/index.md) and [Games](../games/index.md) sections explain the
system, and they link here whenever a detail matters. Come here when you want the full reasoning
behind a decision, the exact rules of a design, or the meaning of a term.

Everything in this section is a plain-language edition of a document in the engineering
repositories. Each page ends with a **Canonical sources** block that links to the original, and
the original wins whenever the two disagree.
